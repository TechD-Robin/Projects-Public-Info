After working with 3D printers and DIY small CNC machines, I returned to the world of Arduino development boards and explored low-power Bluetooth modules (BLE). During this process, I realized that some of the connection steps could be simplified, so I developed this app.

This app was developed based on my own needs, with the goal of simplifying the development process and solving the issues I encountered. I hope it can help developers with similar needs. If it generates some income, that will also help me continue developing such tools.

Since the App Store does not support sponsorship payment models, I chose to implement limitations on the number of automatic connections and data transfers. Users can support development by watching ads or paying to unlock more features.

In any case, I hope this app will help in your BLE development process. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.
